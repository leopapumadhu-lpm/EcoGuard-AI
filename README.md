# 🌍 EcoGuard AI
### AI-Powered Climate Risk, Sustainability & Awareness Assistant

> **"Predict Risk • Reduce Carbon • Understand Climate • Build Resilience"**

Built for the **1M1B AI for Sustainability Virtual Internship** in collaboration with **IBM SkillsBuild** and **AICTE**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

How might we use AI to help individuals and communities understand climate and environmental risks, estimate their carbon footprint, access reliable climate information, and receive practical recommendations?
